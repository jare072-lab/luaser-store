# Brand guides

## Luaser (acrylic laser-cutting, luaser.mx)

**Palette — "Acrílico Neón"** (defined in `tailwind.config.ts` on the site repo):
- `ink` #0B0C0E — primary dark background
- `ink-soft` #16181C, `ink-border` #2A2D33
- `bone` #FAF9F6 — light background/text-on-dark
- `gold` #E8927A (bright #F5AF95, dark #B8654C) — primary accent / CTA, "dorado rosa"
- `pitch` #2E7FE8 (light #5B9FFF) — secondary accent, "azul eléctrico"
- `terracotta` #E63980 — urgency/scarcity only, "rosa neón"
- `graystone` 50–900 — neutral text scale

Logo/wordmark: metallic "LUASER" mark, dark background (see
`PARACLAUDE/752268096...` for an example asset — confirm exact path before reuse, filenames
in that folder are not descriptive).

Voice: confident, premium-craft, precision-focused ("cortado a la décima de milímetro"). Real
photos available: `public/trabajos/*.jpg` (installed signs at real client businesses) and
generated banner/workshop images at `public/generated/*.png`.

## Lúa Eventos (snack/frappé event catering, Monterrey)

**Palette:**
- Deep navy `#1B4A6B` / `#0B1423` — primary background
- White — primary text on navy
- `#E8B39C` / `#F5AF95`-family warm accent for labels/eyebrows (kept close to Luaser's gold
  since both brands share an owner, but treat as a secondary/label accent here, not the
  dominant color — Lúa Eventos reads primarily as navy + white)
- Terracotta/coral `#E63980`-family sparingly for urgency badges (promo countdowns)

Logo: circular badge, "Lúa Eventos — Snacks Party", white brush-script wordmark on navy circle.

## Shared conventions across both brands

- Never invent a new palette per-piece — pull from the above.
- Emoji in composited (SVG-rendered) text render as blank boxes — strip them, don't rely on
  them for tone; use wording or SVG icon shapes instead.
- Circular logo badge with a thin white ring is the established treatment for both brands'
  logos when composited onto a photo — keep using it for consistency rather than a new
  treatment per piece.
